How it works

  1. This is your garden.

    You and your partner share one. It starts quiet — a path, a stream, a few empty plots of soil.

  2. Every empty plot is a thank-you waiting.

    Notice something kind they did today? That’s a seed.

  3. Tap plant, write what you noticed.

    A few words is enough. It takes less time than making tea.

  4. It blooms, with your words on it.

    “You make me laugh” becomes a rose by the path, waiting for your partner to find it.

  5. Over time, it fills up.

    Months later, the garden is a map of everything you love about each other — and something to walk through on a hard day.

Go on, plant one.

Think of one small thing your person did this week. Write it down and watch it grow. Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere.

Small enough to keep doing. Big enough to matter.
