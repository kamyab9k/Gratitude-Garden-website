The idea

The little things
matter most.

Relationships grow in small moments — the coffee made without asking, the hug after a long day, the message that made you smile at your desk. We forget most of them by dinner. Gratitude Garden is a place to keep them.

How it works

Three small habits.
One closer couple.

Plant a gratitude

Write a quick note about something your partner did — it takes ten seconds and means everything.

Watch it grow

Every note becomes a flower in a garden you tend together — a living record of your relationship.

Feel closer, daily

A gentle reminder each evening turns appreciation into a habit — and habits into a stronger us.
